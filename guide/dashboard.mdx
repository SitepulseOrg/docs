The dashboard is your team's health at a glance — recent check results, anything that needs attention, your pinned sites, and shortcuts to common actions, all on one page.

## Health overview

The cards at the top give you the quick read on how your team is doing:

- **Total sites monitored**
- **Uptime this month**
- **Checks run today**
- **Open issues**

Glance at these before diving into individual sites — if open issues are at zero and your pinned sites show a positive [pulse](/guide/sites#pulse-indicator), you're done.

## Attention sections

When something's wrong, the dashboard groups the affected sites so you can jump straight to what's broken:

| Section | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Down sites** | Latest status check says the site is unavailable |
| **SSL issues** | Latest SSL check reports an invalid or risky certificate |
| **DNS issues** | Latest DNS check reports a hard failure or expected-record mismatch |
| **Broken links** | Latest broken-link check found failing URLs |
| **Low performance** | Latest performance check breaches configured thresholds |

Each section takes you straight to the matching [Sites](/guide/sites#health-states) view, and you can collapse any section you don't need right now.

## Pinned sites

Pin your most important sites — production, key clients, a launch you're watching — and they get their own spot on the dashboard with uptime context, so you never have to go searching for them. Pinned sites also appear in the sidebar with a [pulse indicator](/guide/sites#pulse-indicator) that reflects their latest Status, SSL, Broken Links, and Performance results.

Pin or unpin from the [Sites](/guide/sites#pinning) list or from an individual site.

## Quick actions

Shortcuts for the things you do most:

- Add a site
- Set up team monitoring
- Set up notifications
- View issues
- Run a quick on-demand check

Your plan and remaining monthly on-demand check quota decide which check shortcuts are available. Your team role still controls whether you can run them.

## Notification setup reminder

If your team hasn't set up anywhere to send alerts yet, the dashboard nudges you with a reminder (as long as you're allowed to manage notifications). Follow it to **Notifications** to add email, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, or a webhook — and dismiss it once you're set.

## A note on teams

Everything on the dashboard reflects your **current team**. If the numbers look off, check you're in the right team — switch from **Teams** or the team selector at any time.
