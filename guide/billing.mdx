Billing works per team - each one has its own plan and limits, so you can bill a client team separately from your own.

<Note>
  Only the team owner can manage billing.
</Note>

## Free trial

Eligible owners receive 14 days of Starter access when they create a new team after verifying their email. You do not need a payment card. Signing up or accepting an invitation does not start a trial.

The trial includes all five check types and the [Starter limits](#plans): 5 sites, 3 seats including the owner and pending invitations, and 50 on-demand checks per calendar month. Check history follows Starter's rolling 30-day retention policy.

### Eligibility

- You can receive one trial per owner account, on a newly created team.
- None of your owned teams can have previous trial or subscription history. Deleted teams still count.
- Existing teams are not automatically enrolled.
- Joining, switching, deleting, or restoring a team does not grant or restart a trial.

If you do not qualify, choose a paid plan from **Billing** to start monitoring.

### Trial status and reminders

The dashboard notice shows your trial's end date. **Billing** shows the exact deadline in UTC and your remaining usage. Sitepulse emails the owner near the end of the trial and after it expires. The deadline applies even if a reminder is delayed.

The trial is managed by Sitepulse. It does not create a Stripe subscription or renew automatically. You are not charged when the trial expires.

## Billing page

Open **Teams -> Manage current team -> Billing** to review the current plan, usage, and available upgrades or downgrades.

From this page, owners can:

- Choose monthly or yearly pricing
- Start checkout for a plan
- Switch plans or billing period
- Open the Stripe billing portal

## Subscribing during a trial

As the owner, choose any available plan and billing period on **Billing**, then click **Subscribe now**.

<Note>
  Payment starts immediately and replaces the remaining free trial. Unused trial days do not carry over to the subscription.
</Note>

Leaving Checkout before a subscription is created does not change your trial deadline. If a subscription is created but its payment needs confirmation, follow the payment recovery prompts. A failed or canceled subscription does not restore the free trial.

You may briefly see pending activation while Sitepulse processes payment confirmation. Paid access depends on the subscription becoming valid, not only on returning from Checkout.

## Plans

All plans include Status, SSL, DNS, Broken Links, and Performance checks. Paid subscriptions are billed in USD; see [current pricing](https://sitepulse.dev/pricing/) for monthly and yearly amounts.

| Plan | Sites | Seats | On-demand checks / month | Fastest cadence | History retention |
|------|-------|-------|-----------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Starter** | 5 | 3 | 50 | 10 minutes | 30 days |
| **Growth** | 25 | 10 | 250 | 5 minutes | 90 days |
| **Agency** | 100 | 25 | 1000 | 1 minute | 365 days |

<Info>
  Broken-link and performance checks have an additional fastest cadence floor of 12 hours. If your plan allows checks every 1 or 5 minutes, those faster intervals apply to tools such as Status, SSL, and DNS.
</Info>

## Usage limits

Plan limits affect:

- Number of sites on the team
- Number of seats, including pending invitations
- Monthly on-demand check runs
- Fastest automatic check cadence
- Check history retention

Once you hit a limit, the action that would go over it is simply unavailable - you can't add a site when you're at your site limit, invite someone when seats are full, or run more on-demand checks once you've used this month's quota. Upgrading lifts the limit straight away.

## When access ends

When your team has neither an active trial nor a valid subscription, new monitoring pauses. Trial expiry does not delete your sites or settings. Check history continues to age out under the normal retention policy.

Owners are directed to **Billing** to subscribe or resolve payment issues. Other members are directed to **Teams** with a message to contact the owner. Account settings and team switching remain available.

Protected API requests return `402 Payment Required`. See [team access and billing](/api/authentication#team-access-and-billing) for error messages and endpoints that remain available.

Monitoring resumes when the team's paid subscription becomes valid. Canceling a subscription does not grant another trial.
