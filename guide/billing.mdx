Billing is team-scoped. Each team has its own subscription, usage limits, payment method, and invoice history.

<Note>
  Only the team owner can manage billing.
</Note>

## Billing page

Open **Teams -> Manage current team -> Billing** to review the current plan, usage, payment method, invoices, and available upgrades or downgrades.

From this page, owners can:

- Choose monthly or yearly pricing
- Start checkout for a plan
- Switch plans or billing period
- Open the Stripe billing portal
- Download invoice history when invoices are available

## Plans

All plans include Status, SSL, DNS, Broken Links, and Performance checks.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Sites | Seats | Manual checks / month | Fastest cadence | History retention | Priority queue |
|------|---------|--------|-------|-------|-----------------------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Starter** | £10 | £100 | 5 | 3 | 50 | 30 minutes | 30 days | No |
| **Growth** | £25 | £250 | 25 | 10 | 250 | 5 minutes | 90 days | Yes |
| **Agency** | £50 | £500 | 100 | 25 | 1000 | 1 minute | 365 days | Yes |

<Info>
  Broken-link and performance checks have an additional fastest cadence floor of 12 hours. If your plan allows checks every 1 or 5 minutes, those faster intervals apply to tools such as Status, SSL, and DNS.
</Info>

## Usage limits

Plan limits affect:

- Number of sites on the team
- Number of seats, including pending invitations
- Monthly manual check runs
- Fastest automatic check cadence
- Check history retention
- Whether queued checks use priority processing

When a limit is reached, SitePulse disables or rejects the action that would exceed it. For example, you cannot add a site after the site limit is full, invite another member after the seat limit is full, or run manual checks after the monthly manual-check quota is used.

## Subscription access

Team-scoped dashboard and API areas require active plan access when subscription enforcement is enabled. If a subscription is inactive, team-scoped API requests return `402` and dashboard access is limited until billing is resolved.

## Invoices and payment method

The billing page shows the current payment method and recent invoices when Stripe returns them. Use **Manage in Stripe** to update payment details, cancel, or handle other billing portal actions.
