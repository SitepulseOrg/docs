Teams allow you to collaborate with others and share monitoring responsibilities. Each team has its own sites, monitoring settings, billing, and member access controls.

<Info>
  See also [Account and Preferences](/guide/account-settings) for per-user profile, password, passkeys, and appearance settings.
</Info>

## Personal team

When you register, SitePulse creates a **personal team** for your account. This is your default workspace for adding sites and running checks.

- The personal team cannot be deleted
- You can create additional teams for clients or projects
- The API exposes `personal_team: true` on the team resource for your personal workspace

## Creating or joining a team

New users verify their email before creating or joining a team. After verification, SitePulse sends users without a team to team creation unless they have a pending invitation.

Invited users can join from:
- The invitation email
- The invitations page in SitePulse

<Warning>
  An invitation can only be accepted by the email address it was sent to.
</Warning>

## Roles

SitePulse uses three practical access levels:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Manage the team, billing, members, security, monitoring, notifications, sites, checks, and issues.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
    Manage team settings, members, monitoring, notifications, sites, checks, and issues.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Member" icon="user">
    Work with sites, checks, issues, and notifications without managing team administration or billing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Owners** are the only users who can manage billing. Team deletion is owner-only.
</Note>

## Members and invitations

Admins and owners can invite members by email and choose a role. 

**Managing invitations:**

- Pending invitations can be revoked by users with member-management permission
- Invitees can decline their own invitations
- When a member's role changes, SitePulse emails that member so they know their access changed

Open **Manage Team → Members** from the team switcher menu to manage members and invitations.

## Browsing teams

If you belong to multiple teams:

- Use the **team switcher** in the sidebar header for quick switching
- Open **Teams** (`/teams`) from the user menu to see all workspaces you can access and switch context from a full list

## Monitoring

Team monitoring settings define automatic check cadence, issue thresholds, and quiet time. Admins and owners configure these under **Monitoring** in the sidebar.

See the [Monitoring settings](/guide/monitoring) guide for cadence presets, default thresholds, enabled issue tools, DNS expected records, and quiet time.

## Notifications

Admins and owners set up notification destinations under **Notifications** in the sidebar. These control where alerts are sent when issues are detected or resolved, and for manual check results.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email">
    Add email addresses that receive issue alerts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Slack">
    Connect the SitePulse Slack app and choose a channel, or use a Slack incoming webhook URL.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Teams">
    Add a Microsoft Teams incoming webhook URL for issue updates.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Webhook">
    Add generic webhook URLs (GET or POST) for PagerDuty, custom automation, and other integrations.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each notification configuration can subscribe to issue detected and resolved events, target all tools or selected tools only, and be enabled, disabled, tested, edited, or removed at any time.

See the [Notifications](/guide/notifications) guide for full details on channels, events, quiet time, and when alerts fire. For HTTP payload schemas and delivery behavior, see [Webhook notifications](/guide/webhooks).

## Billing

Billing is managed by the team owner under **Manage Team → Billing**. The billing page shows:

- Current plan and available plans
- Plan limits (sites, members, cadence)
- Payment method details
- Invoice history
- Checkout and Stripe billing portal (when available)

### Plans

SitePulse offers three pricing tiers. All plans include all five check tools.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Sites | Seats | Min cadence | History retention | Priority queue |
| :--- | :------ | :----- | :---- | :---- | :---------- | :---------------- | :------------- |
| **Starter** | £10 | £100 | 5 | 3 | 30 minutes | 30 days | No |
| **Growth** | £25 | £250 | 25 | 10 | 5 minutes | 90 days | Yes |
| **Agency** | £50 | £500 | 100 | 25 | 1 minute | 365 days | Yes |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What plan limits affect">
    Plan limits control:
    - Number of sites you can monitor
    - Number of team members
    - Fastest allowed automatic monitoring cadence
    - How long check history is retained
    - Whether checks use the priority queue (Growth and Agency)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

Teams can require two-factor authentication (2FA) for all members. Owners and admins configure this under **Manage Team → General** in the Security section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Team">
    Use the team switcher menu and select **Manage Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable 2FA requirement">
    Toggle **Require two-factor authentication** in the Security section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team members enable 2FA">
    Once enabled, all team members are prompted to set up 2FA before accessing team-scoped dashboard areas.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Users who haven't enabled 2FA will not be able to access team areas until they complete setup.
</Warning>

## Switching teams

If you belong to multiple teams:

1. Use the **team switcher** in the sidebar header (visible when you have more than one team)
2. Select a different team to change your "current team" context
3. Sites, monitoring settings, and billing are always scoped to the current team

<Tip>
  You can leave a team from the switcher menu (owner removal or self-removal rules apply per policy).
</Tip>
